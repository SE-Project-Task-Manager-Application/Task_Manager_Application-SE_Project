Add AuditService and NotificationService stubs (S-05)

AuditService.record(actor, action, target, timestamp) writes append-only rows
to the new AuditRecord model (adminpanel); timestamps are UTC, action names are
validated, and secret-looking detail keys are redacted. NotificationService
provides enqueue/send/deliver_pending as a small in-memory stub that e-mails
through Django's mail layer and never lets a delivery failure escape.
Both are callable from a view and from a test.
